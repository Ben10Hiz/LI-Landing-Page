# Indiana State Representative — 2026 race profile

_Researched 2026-10-10 · status: draft._ Pending editorial review.

## About the office

Members of the Indiana House of Representatives write and vote on state laws as part of the Indiana General Assembly. The House has 100 members, each representing one district for a two-year term [1].

- **Term:** 2 years [1]
- **Term limit:** None [1]
- **Seats:** 100 [1]
- **On the 2026 ballot:** All 100 [2]

### What the office does

- Introduces and votes on state legislation as one of the two chambers of the Indiana General Assembly [1].
- Must be at least 21 years old when taking office and have lived in the district for at least a year [1].

### Recent changes to the office


## Current officeholder: Stephen Bartels (Republican)

Stephen Bartels of Eckerty, an Army veteran and Patoka Lake business owner, was chosen by a Republican caucus in November 2017 to fill the rest of Lloyd Arnold's term and has held House District 74 since [3]. He chairs the House Veterans Affairs and Public Safety Committee [4].

- Committees: Veterans Affairs and Public Safety (chair), Courts and Criminal Code, and Public Policy; co-chair of the legislature's defense caucus (2025) [4]. In May 2026 Gov. Mike Braun picked him to chair the Indiana Defense Task Force [5].
- 2023: Sponsored HEA 1017, creating a prescription drug donation repository for uninsured or underinsured low-income Hoosiers [6].
- 2026: Authored HEA 1003, which sunsets or restructures more than 40 state boards and commissions [7]; authored HEA 1210, raising the property tax deduction for totally disabled veterans to 100% of their home's assessed value and adding other veteran credits [8]; sponsored SEA 227, making the Indiana State Police the lead agency for countering drones once federal authorization exists [9].
- Voted yes on the 2025 budget conference report, HEA 1001 (April 25, 2025) [10]; was excused and did not vote on SEA 1, the 2025 property-tax law, on April 10, 2025 [11]. Was one of the Republicans who voted no on HB 1032, the congressional redistricting bill, which passed the House 57-41 on Dec. 5, 2025 [12].

## This race

- House District 74 covers all of Perry, Crawford and Spencer counties and parts of Dubois and Orange counties, per the House Republican caucus [4].
- Bartels won 68.6% of the vote in 2024 [13]. He was unopposed in the May 5, 2026 Republican primary, and no Democrat appears in the primary results [13].
- The Dubois County Free Press lists Stephen R. Bartels (R) and Lena Goffinet (D) on the Nov. 3 ballot [14].
- Key differences: Goffinet has published no policy positions that we could find; her campaign website shows only a placeholder page [15].
- No debate or forum for this race was found.

## Stephen R. Bartels

Stephen Bartels has held the seat since November 2017 and chairs the House Veterans Affairs and Public Safety Committee [3, 4]. He was unopposed in the May primary [13].

### Background

- Served 22 years in the military, retiring as a major, and nine years as an Indiana law enforcement officer [4].
- Owns and operates Patoka Lake Marina and is owner and vintner of Patoka Lake Winery [4].
- Former Crawford County Council president and former president of the Crawford County Tourism Board [4, 3]. Lives in Eckerty [4].

### Path to the ballot

- Was unopposed in the May 5, 2026 Republican primary [13].

### Positions & plans

**Veterans**

- Authored HEA 1210 (2026) expanding property tax relief for disabled veterans, saying it protects "their homes as a thank you for protecting ours" [8].

**Government operations**

- Authored HEA 1003 (2026) to consolidate state boards and commissions, saying it "strengthens and modernizes" how they are structured [7].

**Public safety**

- Sponsored SEA 227 (2026) on counter-drone authority, saying it "gives law enforcement the tools they need to stay ahead of emerging threats" [9].

**Economy & jobs**

- Says defense-industry growth in southern Indiana is creating high-wage careers [5].

**Elections & redistricting**

- Voted no on the December 2025 congressional redistricting bill, HB 1032 [12].

### On the current officeholder

- Is the current officeholder. No statements about his challenger were found.

### How they say they'd run the office

- As chair of the Indiana Defense Task Force, his 2026 bill directs state defense funding to match the task force's priorities [5].

- No public statement explaining his no vote on HB 1032 was found.
