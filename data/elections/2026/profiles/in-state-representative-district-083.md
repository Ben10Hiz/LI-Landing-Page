# Indiana State Representative — 2026 race profile

_Researched 2026-10-10 · status: draft._ Pending editorial review.

## About the office

Members of the Indiana House of Representatives write and vote on state laws as part of the Indiana General Assembly. The House has 100 members, each representing one district for a two-year term [1].

- **Term:** 2 years [1]
- **Term limit:** None [1]
- **Seats:** 100 [1]
- **On the 2026 ballot:** All 100 [2]

### What the office does

- Introduces and votes on state legislation as one of the two chambers of the Indiana General Assembly [1].
- Must be at least 21 years old when taking office and have lived in the district for at least a year [1].

### Recent changes to the office


## Current officeholder: Christopher Judy (Republican)

Christopher "Chris" Judy, an insurance broker and Indiana National Guard veteran who served in Iraq as a combat medic, has represented House District 83 since September 2014 [3]. He serves on the Ways and Means, Financial Institutions, and Veterans Affairs and Public Safety committees [3].

- Committees: Financial Institutions; Veterans Affairs and Public Safety; Ways and Means, according to the House Republican caucus [3]. His campaign says he has served on Ways and Means since 2021 [4].
- 2024: Supported HEA 1021, which created a Green Alert for missing at-risk veterans and service members [5]. 2025: Co-sponsored SEA 520, waiving a $100,000 deposit requirement so Fort Wayne's Veterans National Memorial Shrine and Museum could build a columbarium [6].
- 2026: Co-authored SEA 256 (disclosure requirements for people acting for designated foreign adversaries) and HEA 1202 (ignition interlock and other public-safety updates) [7]. Supported HEA 1210, which replaces the $14,000 deduction for totally disabled veterans with a full deduction on their homes and converts other veteran deductions to credits [8].
- Spring 2026: Said he had raised concerns about the location of a proposed quarry in southwest Allen County near Lafayette Meadows Elementary and IU Health facilities, while noting land-use decisions are made locally [9]. The Allen County Board of Zoning Appeals denied the rezoning on May 26, 2026, and the developer withdrew its petition [10].
- Voted yes on SEA 1, the 2025 property-tax law (April 10, 2025) [11]; the 2025 budget conference report, HEA 1001 (April 25, 2025) [12]; and HB 1032, the congressional redistricting bill (Dec. 5, 2025) [13].

## This race

- House District 83 covers parts of Allen and Whitley counties, according to the House Republican caucus [3]; Haffenden's campaign describes it as including Columbia City, South Whitley and southwest Fort Wayne [14].
- Judy was reelected with 64% of the vote in 2024 [15]. Both nominees were unopposed in the May 5, 2026 primaries [15].
- Local issue: a proposed limestone quarry on more than 800 acres between Homestead and Yohne roads in southwest Allen County drew opposition from residents, Southwest Allen County Schools and the Little River Wetlands Project [16]; the rezoning was denied in May 2026 [10]. Both candidates commented on it [9, 17].
- Key differences: Judy lists small business, veterans and balanced budgets with low taxes as priorities [4]; Haffenden proposes a $15 minimum wage, ending school vouchers, a pause on new data centers, independent redistricting and cannabis legalization [18].
- No debate or forum for this race was found.

## Christopher Judy

Incumbent since 2014; an insurance broker and Iraq veteran who serves on the House Ways and Means Committee [3, 4].

Campaign website: https://christopherjudyforindiana.com/

### Background

- Licensed life and health insurance broker; his campaign says he has worked with Tradewell Tax and Financial since 2018 and in business development at TJ Nowak Supply since 2024 [4].
- Indiana National Guard veteran who deployed to Iraq in 2008 as a combat medic; member of Action Over Silence, a Fort Wayne nonprofit focused on suicide prevention among service members and veterans [3].
- Raised mostly in Whitley County; lives in Aboite Township [3].

### Path to the ballot

- Unopposed in the May 5, 2026 Republican primary [15].

### Positions & plans

**Economy & jobs**

- Says he would "protect the farmers, employers, and entrepreneurs who form the backbone of the Hoosier economy" and push back on unnecessary regulation [4].
- Says he wants to "Keep Indiana's budget balanced, spending responsible, and taxes low" [4].

**Veterans**

- Says he will advocate for "service members, veterans, and Gold Star families" [4]. On HEA 1210: "This new law strengthens long-term property tax relief for disabled veterans" [8].

**Public safety**

- Said of the 2026 public-safety laws he backed: "These new laws are about protecting Hoosiers and making sure Indiana stays ahead of the challenges" [7].

**Land use**

- On the proposed quarry, encouraged residents to attend public meetings and said he would remain engaged as the local process continued [9].

### On the current officeholder

- Is the current officeholder. His campaign website does not mention his challenger [4].

### How they say they'd run the office

- His campaign lists small business, veterans and fiscal discipline as priorities [4].

### Endorsements & fundraising

- The caucus lists awards including 2023 Legislator of the Year from the Indiana State Police Alliance and from Disabled American Veterans [3]. No endorsements or fundraising figures for 2026 were verified.

### What we couldn't verify

- A search-result snippet listed Judy as a co-author of Rep. Bob Morris's 2026 HB 1287 (execution team bill); not confirmed on a fetched page.
- No statements on data centers, property taxes generally or redistricting beyond his recorded votes were found.
- Campaign finance totals not checked.

## Wesley Haffenden

Wesley Haffenden is a real estate broker with an MBA who was raised in rural northeast Indiana [19]. He was unopposed in the Democratic primary [15].

Campaign website: https://www.wesleyhaffenden.com/

### Background

- Real estate broker with nearly a decade of experience; holds a bachelor's degree in economics and an MBA [19].
- Raised in rural northeast Indiana; lived in Florida for about six years after 2015 for work [19]. Says he co-created Fort Wayne's first Pride march [19].

### Path to the ballot

- Unopposed in the May 5, 2026 Democratic primary [15].

### Positions & plans

**Economy & jobs**

- Proposes a $15 minimum wage in 2027 tied to inflation afterward, at least seven paid sick days a year, and paid family leave [18].

**Education**

- Proposes raising starting teacher pay to $60,000 over three years, expanding pre-K and phasing out the voucher system [18].

**Property taxes**

- Says school funding should "reduce over-reliance on property taxes" [18].

**Health care**

- Supports upfront pricing and no surprise billing at the state level and says "I support Medicare for All" [18].

**Energy & utilities**

- Proposes capping delivery fees, tying costs to actual usage and a utility customer bill of rights [18].

**Data centers**

- Proposes pausing approval of new large data centers and eliminating "taxpayer subsidies, tax abatements, and incentives for data centers" [18].

**Housing**

- Proposes limiting large-scale out-of-state corporate home buying and increasing affordable supply [18].

**Elections & redistricting**

- Supports independent redistricting, citizen ballot initiatives, term limits, automatic voter registration and real-time campaign donor disclosure [18].

**Land use**

- Opposed the southwest Allen County quarry, saying heavy industry in a floodplain near homes, schools and hospitals "just does not make sense" [17].

**Social issues**

- Supports legalizing adult-use cannabis, universal background checks on gun sales, protecting personal medical decisions and constitutional protection for same-sex marriage [18].

### On the current officeholder

- In April 2026 he said: "I'm challenging my opponent directly to join me in speaking out against this industrial project," referring to the quarry [17]. His homepage closes with a call to "defeat Chris Judy" but does not discuss Judy's record [14].

### How they say they'd run the office

- Says his goal is to "make government answer to the people, not the highest bidder" [14].

### Endorsements & fundraising

- His campaign lists the Moms Demand Action "Gun Sense Candidate" distinction, Planned Parenthood Alliance Advocates, the Indiana Coalition for Public Education, and a signed term-limits pledge [20]. No fundraising figures were verified.

### What we couldn't verify

- No independent news coverage of his campaign was found.
- His website does not name the city where he lives.
- Campaign finance totals not checked.
