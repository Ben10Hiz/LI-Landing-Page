# Indiana State Representative — 2026 race profile

_Researched 2026-10-10 · status: draft._ Pending editorial review.

## About the office

Members of the Indiana House of Representatives write and vote on state laws as part of the Indiana General Assembly. The House has 100 members, each representing one district for a two-year term [1].

- **Term:** 2 years [1]
- **Term limit:** None [1]
- **Seats:** 100 [1]
- **On the 2026 ballot:** All 100 [2]

### What the office does

- Introduces and votes on state legislation as one of the two chambers of the Indiana General Assembly [1].
- Must be at least 21 years old when taking office and have lived in the district for at least a year [1].

### Recent changes to the office


## Current officeholder: Alex Zimmerman (Republican)

Alex Zimmerman, a North Vernon attorney, has represented House District 67 since July 2023, when a Republican caucus chose him to fill the seat left by Rep. Randy Frye; he was elected unopposed in 2024 [3, 4, 5]. He is vice chair of the House Judiciary Committee [3].

- Committees: Judiciary (vice chair), Courts and Criminal Code, and Rules and Legislative Procedures, according to his caucus biography [3].
- 2024: Authored two amendments, which failed, that would have barred federal funding for INDOT's Link 101 Corridor highway project and required the agency to consider improving existing roads first; when INDOT canceled the project on May 1, 2024, he said constituents had raised concerns about farmland and "generational homesteads" [6].
- 2025: Authored HEA 1114, which makes driving without ever having obtained a license a Level 5 or 6 felony when it causes bodily injury, catastrophic injury or death; he said he wrote it after a Holton man was killed in a head-on crash with an unlicensed driver [7, 8]. Also authored HEA 1014, which caps total incarceration for consecutive misdemeanor sentences, and HEA 1079; all three were ceremonially signed by Gov. Mike Braun [8].
- 2026: House sponsor of SEA 285, which prohibits unauthorized camping or sleeping in public spaces and requires police to give first-time offenders a warning and help with shelter and services before issuing a citation; Zimmerman called it "a balanced approach" [9].
- Voted yes on SEA 1, the 2025 property-tax law (April 10, 2025) [10]; the 2025 budget conference report, HEA 1001 (April 25, 2025) [11]; and HB 1032, the congressional redistricting bill (Dec. 5, 2025) [12].

## This race

- House District 67 covers portions of Decatur, Jefferson, Jennings and Ripley counties in southeast Indiana, according to the House Republican caucus [3]. (It does not include Dearborn or Franklin counties.)
- Zimmerman was appointed by a Republican caucus in July 2023 after Rep. Randy Frye resigned, and was elected without opposition in 2024 [4, 5].
- Both candidates were the only filers in their party's May 5, 2026 primary [5, 13].
- No debate or candidate forum for this race was found.

## Alex Zimmerman

Incumbent Republican and North Vernon attorney seeking his second full term [3, 5].

Campaign website: https://www.zimmermanforindiana.com/

### Background

- Attorney with a private law practice in North Vernon; earned his law degree from the IU Robert H. McKinney School of Law [3].
- Previously worked for the Indiana Senate as a legislative assistant, majority legal associate and deputy majority attorney [3].
- Lives in North Vernon [3].

### Path to the ballot

- Filed for reelection and was the only Republican in the May 5, 2026 primary [13, 5].

### Positions & plans

**Public safety**

- Says HEA 1114 is meant as "a deterrent to ensure those who do not have a license refrain from getting behind the wheel" [7].
- On SEA 285 (2026), said the law prioritizes "intervention and support" for people experiencing homelessness, "with any potential enforcement action as a last resort" [9].

**Transportation**

- Opposed INDOT's proposed Link 101 Corridor through Ripley and neighboring counties and welcomed its cancellation in 2024 [6].
