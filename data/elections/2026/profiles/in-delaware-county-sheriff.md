## Current officeholder: Tony Skinner (Republican)

Tony Skinner, a Republican, was elected sheriff in November 2018 and is finishing his second four-year term, which ends in December 2026; state law bars him from seeking a third consecutive term, and he is running for the Delaware County Council District 3 seat instead [2, 3].

- November 2018: Skinner, then a sergeant in the sheriff's office, defeated Democratic incumbent Sheriff Ray Dudley with 52.7% of the vote; he said he would target large-scale drug dealers and traffickers and take politics out of hiring and promotion decisions [4].
- February 2019: The county council voted 4–3 to fund a $45 million justice center in the former Wilson Middle School, including a 500-bed jail; Skinner said the existing jail held more than 340 inmates in a space built for 220 and that the project, while unpopular, was needed [5].
- February 2021: The sheriff's office moved 235 inmates into the new facility; Skinner said the old building had been 'completely maxed out' and that the county had been housing inmates in other counties' jails [6].
- July 2023: The department's financial officer told the county council the jail had 10 corrections-officer openings; the department had spent more than $100,000 on overtime to cover shortages in 2021 and advertised starting pay of $38,166 [7].
- December 2025: Skinner announced his campaign for county council, citing his experience with the county budgeting process [2].

## This race

- This is an open seat: Sheriff Tony Skinner is term-limited and is running for county council [2].
- The Delaware County Election Board removed Republican James McCall from the May 5 primary ballot on Feb. 24, 2026; County Clerk Rick Spanger said McCall had not voted in two prior Republican primaries in Indiana, as required to run in a GOP primary. ## Jeff Stanley

Jeff Stanley is chief deputy of the Delaware County Sheriff's Department, with more than 30 years in county law enforcement; he became the Republican nominee after his only primary opponent was removed from the ballot [3, 9].

Campaign website: https://jeffstanleyforsheriff.com

### Background

- Chief deputy of the Delaware County Sheriff's Department under Sheriff Skinner [2, 3].
- His campaign says he has served Delaware County for more than 30 years in patrol, criminal investigations, SWAT, the bomb squad, search and recovery, and executive leadership as chief deputy [9].

### Path to the ballot

- Announced his candidacy by December 2025 [2]. Became the Republican nominee after the county election board removed James McCall from the May 5, 2026 primary ballot [3].

### Positions & plans

**Patrol & response**

- Supports 'proactive policing that targets violent criminals, repeat offenders' and says deputies need training, equipment and support; pledges to enforce the law 'without political influence' [9].

**Jail & corrections**

- Supports jail programs in education, job readiness, substance-abuse recovery, peer support and vocational training to reduce repeat offenses, saying rehabilitation 'should never replace accountability' [9].

**Budget**

**School safety**

- Says the sheriff's office should be present in schools and at community events, and pledges to expand community outreach [9].
