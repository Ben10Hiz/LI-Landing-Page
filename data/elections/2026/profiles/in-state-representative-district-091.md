# Indiana State Representative — 2026 race profile

_Researched 2026-10-10 · status: draft._ Pending editorial review.

## About the office

Members of the Indiana House of Representatives write and vote on state laws as part of the Indiana General Assembly. The House has 100 members, each representing one district for a two-year term [1].

- **Term:** 2 years [1]
- **Term limit:** None [1]
- **Seats:** 100 [1]
- **On the 2026 ballot:** All 100 [2]

### What the office does

- Introduces and votes on state legislation as one of the two chambers of the Indiana General Assembly [1].
- Must be at least 21 years old when taking office and have lived in the district for at least a year [1].

### Recent changes to the office


## Current officeholder: Bob Behning (Republican)

Bob Behning (Robert W. Behning on the ballot), a Republican from Indianapolis, has served in the Indiana House since 1992 and chairs the House Education Committee; he also serves on the Public Health Committee [3, 4]. The House Republican caucus says he authored the 2011 law creating Indiana's school voucher program and works at Marian University as director of its Center for Vibrant Schools [3].

- December 2024: Reappointed chair of the House Education Committee; he said the committee would "continue our work to empower parents to send their students to the school of their choice" [4].
- January 2025: In a column distributed by the caucus, urged moving the Choice Scholarship (voucher) program to universal eligibility by "eliminating the remaining income cap" [5].
- April 2025: Voted for SB 1, the property-tax and local government finance bill, which passed the House 65–29 [6], and for the conference report on HEA 1001, the 2025–2027 state budget, adopted 66–27 [7].
- May 2025: Authored HEA 1002, which the caucus says cut nearly 10% of the education code by removing outdated and duplicate provisions, unused programs and some pandemic-era mandates [8].
- December 5, 2025: Voted for HB 1032, the mid-decade congressional redistricting bill, which passed the House 57–41 [9].
- January 28, 2026: Voted for HB 1002, the electric utility affordability bill, which passed the House 89–4 [10].
- 2026 session: Authored HEA 1004, which the caucus says removed further outdated K-12 requirements and directs the State Board of Education to plan for career and technical education [11]. Authored HEA 1408, which the caucus says requires parental consent and monitoring tools for minors on social media and limits certain addictive features [12].

## This race

- House District 91 covers a portion of Marion County, in central Indiana, according to the House Republican caucus [3].
- Behning was re-elected in 2024 with 60.1% of the vote [13].
- Behning and Hurt were each the only candidate listed in their party's May 5, 2026 primary [13].
- No debate or forum between the candidates was found as of October 10, 2026; neither had answered Mirror Indy's voter-guide questions [14, 15].
- Education is the main contrast: Behning authored the state's voucher program and has pushed universal eligibility, while Hurt's campaign calls for fully funding public schools, universal pre-K and free school lunch [3, 5, 16].

## Jarren Hurt

Jarren Hurt, the Democratic nominee, is a Navy submarine veteran, real estate agent and owner of a fiber-optic installation business, according to his campaign [16].

Campaign website: https://jhurt91.com/

### Background

- His campaign describes him as a Navy submarine veteran, Eagle Scout and Indiana University graduate who works as a real estate agent and runs a utility fiber-optic installation company [16].

### Path to the ballot

- Was the only Democratic candidate listed for the May 5, 2026 primary [13].

### Positions & plans

**Education**

- Would fully fund public schools, support teachers, and back universal pre-K and free K-12 school lunch, according to his campaign [16].

**Data centers**

- Opposes subsidizing large technology companies at families' expense and would push back on data center projects that harm communities or the environment [16].

**Transportation**

- Supports cutting Indiana's gas tax and eliminating toll roads [16].

**Economy & jobs**

- Supports raising the minimum wage [16].

**Housing**

- Would advocate for more affordable housing supply and keeping homeownership within reach [16].

### On the current officeholder

- No statements by Hurt about Behning or his record were found on his campaign website or in voter guides [16, 15].

### What we couldn't verify

- No news coverage, endorsements or campaign finance reports were found.
- Hurt had not answered Mirror Indy's voter-guide questions as of Oct. 10, 2026.
- Where he lives is not stated on his campaign site.

## Robert W. Behning

Robert W. (Bob) Behning is the Republican incumbent, in the House since 1992, and chairs the House Education Committee [3].

### Background

- Born and raised in Indianapolis; Indiana University graduate (1976); assistant vice president and director of the Center for Vibrant Schools at Marian University, according to the caucus [3].

### Path to the ballot

- Was the only Republican candidate listed for the May 5, 2026 primary [13].

### Positions & plans

**Education**

- Has advocated for universal private-school choice, writing that the program lets parents choose "regardless of income or ZIP code" [5], and has authored laws reducing state K-12 regulations; he said schools "should have the freedom to set their own requirements" [8, 11].

**Children & technology**

- Said his 2026 social media law gives parents "more control over their children's social media use" [12].

### On the current officeholder

- Is the current officeholder. No statements by Behning about his challenger were found [3].

### What we couldn't verify

- His campaign website (robertbehning.com) did not load; no 2026 campaign statements, endorsements or finance totals were found.
- Behning had not answered Mirror Indy's voter-guide questions as of Oct. 10, 2026.
- Which Marion County townships the district currently includes was not confirmed from a source read for this profile (older sources describe Decatur Township and parts of Hendricks and Morgan counties under pre-2021 maps).
- The 2025 budget's change to voucher eligibility was not confirmed from a source read for this profile.
