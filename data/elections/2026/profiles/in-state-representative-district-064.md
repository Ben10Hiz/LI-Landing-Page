# Indiana State Representative — 2026 race profile

_Researched 2026-10-10 · status: draft._ Pending editorial review.

## About the office

Members of the Indiana House of Representatives write and vote on state laws as part of the Indiana General Assembly. The House has 100 members, each representing one district for a two-year term [1].

- **Term:** 2 years [1]
- **Term limit:** None [1]
- **Seats:** 100 [1]
- **On the 2026 ballot:** All 100 [2]

### What the office does

- Introduces and votes on state legislation as one of the two chambers of the Indiana General Assembly [1].
- Must be at least 21 years old when taking office and have lived in the district for at least a year [1].

### Recent changes to the office


## Current officeholder: Matt Hostettler (Republican)

Matt Hostettler, a Republican from Patoka, has represented House District 64 since 2018 and is vice chair of the House Employment, Labor and Pensions Committee; he also serves on the Natural Resources and Public Health committees, according to the House Republican caucus [3].

- 2022 special session: Says on his campaign site that he authored HJR 1, a proposed state constitutional amendment on abortion [4].
- February 2024: Co-authored a bill providing a 13th check to retired public employees, which the House approved unanimously, according to the caucus [3].
- January 2025: Introduced a bill to appropriate additional funding to the Indiana Department of Transportation for roadside mowing along U.S. 41 from the Vanderburgh–Gibson county line to the Knox–Sullivan county line [5].
- On April 10, 2025, voted for SB 1, the property-tax and local government finance bill, which passed the House 65–29 [6]. On April 25, 2025, voted for the conference report on HEA 1001, the 2025–2027 state budget, adopted 66–27 [7].
- December 5, 2025: Voted for HB 1032, the mid-decade congressional redistricting bill, which passed the House 57–41 [8].
- February 19, 2026: Voted to concur in Senate changes to HB 1002, the electric utility affordability bill (94–2) [9].
- January 2026: Co-authored HB 1376 on prohibited food additives, which was referred to the Public Health Committee; no further action is listed [10].
- June 2026: The caucus reported that HEA 1210, which he supported, was ceremonially signed; it lets veterans with a 100% disability rating exclude the full assessed value of their home from property tax and adds new credits for other veterans. Hostettler said the bill "expands property tax relief for veterans, especially those living with disabilities" [11].

## This race

- House District 64 includes portions of Gibson, Knox and Vanderburgh counties in southwest Indiana, according to the House Republican caucus [3]. The Ballot Brief notes that some candidate descriptions also list portions of Pike and Posey counties; voters can confirm their district at indianavoters.in.gov [12].
- Hostettler has held the seat since 2018 and was re-elected without opposition in 2024, according to Wikipedia [13].
- Hostettler and Greer each appear as the only declared candidates in their parties' May 5, 2026 primaries in Wikipedia's listing [13]. WNIN reported in February 2026 that Greer's filing gave Hostettler a general-election challenger [14].
- No debate or forum between the two candidates was found as of October 10, 2026. Both appeared at a Gibson County Ag Day breakfast in March 2026 [15].
- Housing is a stated focus for Greer, while Hostettler's campaign site emphasizes abortion, gun rights and fiscal policy [15, 16].

## Candace (Candy) Greer

Candy Greer, the Democratic nominee, lives in Princeton and manages a mobile home park there, according to the Princeton Daily Clarion [15].

### Background

- Manages Sandy Acres mobile home park in Princeton and has highlighted her work cleaning up that housing development on Princeton's west side, according to the Princeton Daily Clarion [15].
- Lives in Princeton [15].

### Path to the ballot

## Matt Hostettler

The Republican incumbent, in the House since 2018 and vice chair of its Employment, Labor and Pensions Committee [3].

Campaign website: https://www.matthostettler.com

### Background

- A lifelong southwest Indiana resident, North Posey High School graduate and Purdue University management graduate, according to the caucus [3].
- Lives in Patoka in Gibson County [3, 16].

### Path to the ballot

- Wikipedia lists him as the only declared candidate in the May 5, 2026 Republican primary [13].

### Positions & plans

**Abortion**

- Describes himself on his campaign site as "100% Pro-Life" and cites his authorship of HJR 1 in 2022 [16, 4].

**Gun rights**

- Says he co-authored the law allowing handgun carry without a license [16].

**Taxes**

- Says he co-authored what his site calls the largest tax cut in state history and voted for the automatic taxpayer refund of more than $545 million [16].

**Veterans**

- Said HEA 1210 (2026) was about "ensuring more of our veterans can afford to stay in their homes" [11].

**Economy & jobs**

- His campaign site says businesses should not be shut down by "edicts out of Indianapolis" [4].

### On the current officeholder

- Is the current officeholder. No statements by Hostettler about his challenger were found [16].

### How they say they'd run the office

- His campaign site says he wants to keep Indiana competitive and fiscally sound [16].

### Endorsements & fundraising

- His campaign site says he has been endorsed by gun organizations but does not name them [4].

### What we couldn't verify

- No local news coverage of his 2026 campaign was found.
- No public statements explaining his votes on SB 1, the 2025 budget or HB 1032 were found.
- A BillTrack50 scorecard (search snippet only) lists him absent for two Feb. 26, 2026 concurrence votes — unverified.
- No fundraising totals found.
