# Indiana State Representative — 2026 race profile

_Researched 2026-10-10 · status: draft._ Pending editorial review.

## About the office

Members of the Indiana House of Representatives write and vote on state laws as part of the Indiana General Assembly. The House has 100 members, each representing one district for a two-year term [1].

- **Term:** 2 years [1]
- **Term limit:** None [1]
- **Seats:** 100 [1]
- **On the 2026 ballot:** All 100 [2]

### What the office does

- Introduces and votes on state legislation as one of the two chambers of the Indiana General Assembly [1].
- Must be at least 21 years old when taking office and have lived in the district for at least a year [1].

### Recent changes to the office


## Current officeholder: Jim Lucas (Republican)

Jim Lucas, a Seymour business owner and Marine Corps veteran, has represented House District 69 since 2012 [3]. He was reelected in 2024 with 68.7% of the vote [4].

- Committees: Financial Institutions, Veterans Affairs and Public Safety, and Public Policy, according to his caucus biography [3].
- 2023: Authored a law creating an optional, state-funded 40-hour firearms training course for school staff who are allowed to carry [5]. 2024: Authored HEA 1142, creating the Hoosier First Responder Medal of Honor [6]. 2025: Co-authored HEA 1114, raising penalties for unlicensed drivers who cause injury or death [7].
- Voted yes on SEA 1, the 2025 property-tax law (April 10, 2025) [8]; the 2025 budget conference report, HEA 1001 (April 25, 2025) [9]; and HB 1032, the congressional redistricting bill (Dec. 5, 2025) [10].

## This race

- House District 69 includes portions of Bartholomew, Jackson, Scott and Washington counties, according to the House Republican caucus [3]; The Indiana Citizen describes it as representing Seymour, Brownstown and Salem [11]. (It does not include Jennings County, which is mostly in House District 67.)
- Lucas and Chris Bowen were each the only declared candidate in their party's May 5, 2026 primary [4]. Tyler J. Henkle is on the ballot as an independent, making this a three-candidate race [2].
- No debate or candidate forum for this race was found.

## Jim Lucas

Seven-term Republican incumbent from Seymour seeking reelection [3, 4].

### Background

- U.S. Marine Corps veteran and small-business owner (The Awning Guy, Inc.); formerly served on the Seymour City Council [3].
- Lives in Seymour [3].

### Path to the ballot

- Unopposed in the May 5, 2026 Republican primary [4].

### Positions & plans

**Public safety**

- His caucus biography lists "protecting the right to bear arms" among his top priorities, along with jobs, fiscal integrity and education [3].
- On school staff firearms training: "I want our schools to have every available option to boost security and be a deterrent to anyone looking to do harm" [5].
- Told students in 2024 that more gun laws would not stop people "who aren't being stopped by the hundreds of existing laws right now" [12].

## Chris Bowen

Democratic nominee from Seymour who filed in January 2026 as part of the Indiana Rural Summit coalition's candidate filing event [11].

### Background

- Lives in Seymour [11].

### Path to the ballot

- Filed Jan. 7, 2026 with the Indiana Rural Summit, a nonprofit that trains Democratic candidates from rural and small-town Indiana [11]; unopposed in the May 5 Democratic primary [4].

### Positions & plans

**Abortion**

- Said she was motivated to run by the legislature's passage of laws restricting abortion [11].

**Elections & redistricting**

- Cited laws she said limit access to the ballot box as a reason for running [11].

### On the current officeholder

- Said Lucas does not listen to constituents' concerns and "just does what he wants" (as paraphrased by The Indiana Citizen); said, "Even if I don't agree, if that's what most of my constituents want, that's the way I'll vote" [11].
