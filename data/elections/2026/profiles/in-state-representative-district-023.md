# Indiana State Representative — 2026 race profile

_Researched 2026-10-10 · status: draft._ Pending editorial review.

## About the office

Members of the Indiana House of Representatives write and vote on state laws as part of the Indiana General Assembly. The House has 100 members, each representing one district for a two-year term [1].

- **Term:** 2 years [1]
- **Term limit:** None [1]
- **Seats:** 100 [1]
- **On the 2026 ballot:** All 100 [2]

### What the office does

- Introduces and votes on state legislation as one of the two chambers of the Indiana General Assembly [1].
- Must be at least 21 years old when taking office and have lived in the district for at least a year [1].

### Recent changes to the office


## Current officeholder: Ethan Manning (Republican)

Ethan Manning, a Republican real estate broker, auctioneer and cattle farmer from rural Cass County, was first elected in 2018 and chairs the House Public Policy Committee, according to the House Republican caucus [3].

- Committee assignments listed by the caucus: Public Policy (chair), Employment, Labor and Pensions, and Rules and Legislative Procedures [3].
- April 10, 2025: voted no on Senate Bill 1, the property-tax bill, when it passed the House 65–29 on third reading; the roll call shows him as one of three Republicans, with Reps. Haggard and Ireland, voting no [4].
- April 2025: voted yes on the 2025–2027 state budget conference report (HEA 1001), adopted by the House 66–27 [5].
- December 5, 2025: voted yes on House Bill 1032, the mid-decade congressional redistricting map, which passed the House 57–41 [6, 7].
- 2026 session: sponsored House Bill 1052, which bans online sweepstakes casino games; it passed the House 87–11 and the Senate 37–8 and was signed by Gov. Mike Braun in March 2026, with civil penalties of up to $100,000 for operators [8, 9].
- January 2026: authored House Bill 1078 to let the Hoosier Lottery sell tickets online; his committee advanced it 9–3, and a bill tracker shows no House final-passage vote after it was amended on second reading January 13 [8, 10].
- Said in January 2026 that "if i-gaming were legal here … there wouldn't be much market for the sweeps casinos," and supports legalizing online gambling at the state's casinos, per the Indiana Capital Chronicle [8].

## This race

- House District 23 covers portions of Cass and Miami counties, including Logansport and Peru [3, 11]. Manning is seeking re-election; it is not an open seat [2].
- Manning and Austin Meives were each listed as the only declared candidate in their party for the May 5, 2026 primary, according to Wikipedia; Manning won re-election with 73.9% in 2024 [12].
- Meives appeared at a candidate spotlight event in Logansport hosted by the Cass County Democrats in August 2026 [11]. No debate between the two candidates was found as of October 10, 2026.
- Manning's record includes gaming legislation from the Public Policy Committee and a no vote on the 2025 property-tax bill [8, 4]; no published issue positions from Meives were found, so policy differences could not be documented.

## Ethan Manning

Ethan Manning, the Republican incumbent since 2018, chairs the House Public Policy Committee and previously served on the Miami County Council, including as its president [3].

Campaign website: https://electethan.com/

### Background

- Associate broker and auctioneer with Carriger Oldfather Realty, owner of Manning Auctions and part owner of his family's cattle farm, per the caucus [3].
- Served on the Miami County Council from 2014, as president from 2016 to 2018, and on the state Board of Registration for Professional Engineers after a 2015 appointment by Gov. Mike Pence [3].
- Lives in rural Cass County [3].

### Path to the ballot

- Was the only Republican candidate listed for District 23 ahead of the May 5, 2026 primary [12].

### Positions & plans

**Economy & jobs**

- His campaign site says he will "always fight for our small towns, small schools, local businesses, and our agricultural industry" and "will promote fiscal responsibility" [15].

**Gaming**

- Supports legalizing online casino gambling at the state's casinos and carried the 2026 sweepstakes-casino ban [8, 9].

**Education & health care**

- His campaign site says he authored bills to increase funding for small and rural schools and expand rural health care access [15].

**Agriculture**

- On a 2026 law easing rules for local food vendors, said "As a member of a farm family myself, I know how important access to markets is for the agricultural economy" [16].

**Other**

- His campaign site says he "stands for life at all stages" and "will always defend our Second Amendment rights" [15].

### On the current officeholder

- Is the current officeholder. No public statements by Manning about Meives were found.

### Endorsements & fundraising

- His campaign site lists endorsements from Indiana Farm Bureau AgELECT, the Indiana Chamber of Commerce, the NRA, Indiana Right to Life and the Indiana Manufacturers Association [15].
- iVoterGuide lists recent contributions received from groups including GOPAC, Good To Be Free PAC and Hoosiers for Quality Education (2024), without amounts [17].

### What we couldn't verify

- No explanation from Manning for his April 2025 no vote on SB 1 was found.

- The specific rural school funding and rural health care bills his campaign site cites were not identified.
- Did not answer the iVoterGuide questionnaire; no campaign-finance totals were reviewed.
- The final status of HB 1078 (online lottery) after January 2026 was not confirmed.
