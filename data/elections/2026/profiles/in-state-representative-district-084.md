# Indiana State Representative — 2026 race profile

_Researched 2026-10-10 · status: draft._ Pending editorial review.

## About the office

Members of the Indiana House of Representatives write and vote on state laws as part of the Indiana General Assembly. The House has 100 members, each representing one district for a two-year term [1].

- **Term:** 2 years [1]
- **Term limit:** None [1]
- **Seats:** 100 [1]
- **On the 2026 ballot:** All 100 [2]

### What the office does

- Introduces and votes on state legislation as one of the two chambers of the Indiana General Assembly [1].
- Must be at least 21 years old when taking office and have lived in the district for at least a year [1].

### Recent changes to the office


## Current officeholder: Bob Morris (Republican)

Bob Morris, a Fort Wayne small-business owner, has represented House District 84 since 2010 and chairs the House Commerce, Small Business and Economic Development Committee [3]. In 2026 he filed a bill to require that execution teams be made up of volunteer legislators, saying he opposes the death penalty [4].

- Committees: Commerce, Small Business and Economic Development (chair); Employment, Labor and Pensions; Utilities, Energy and Telecommunications, according to the House Republican caucus [3].
- 2026: Filed HB 1287, requiring the state prison warden to select an execution team only from General Assembly members who volunteer, with their names public [4]. Morris said, "Don't give anyone a job that you wouldn't want to do yourself" [4]. WANE reported he came out against the death penalty in 2024 and that his earlier repeal bill, HB 1030, received no committee hearing [5].
- 2026: Co-authored HEA 1424, described by the caucus as supporting farmers and small businesses [6].
- Voted yes on SEA 1, the 2025 property-tax law (April 10, 2025) [7] and HB 1032, the congressional redistricting bill (Dec. 5, 2025) [8]. Was recorded as excused on the April 25, 2025 vote adopting the HEA 1001 budget conference report [9].

## This race

- House District 84 is in Allen County and includes part of Fort Wayne and most of St. Joe Township, according to the House Republican caucus [3].
- Morris was reelected unopposed in 2024 [10]. Both nominees were unopposed in the May 5, 2026 primaries [10].
- Key differences: Morris's legislative focus includes commerce and small business, and he joined other northeast Indiana legislators in highlighting a state utility-affordability review [3, 11]; Meehan's campaign focuses on cost of living, housing, child care and corporate tax breaks [12].

## Bob Morris

Incumbent since 2010 and chair of the House Commerce, Small Business and Economic Development Committee [3].

### Background

- Small-business owner who has founded several ventures in the district; B.S. from Indiana University [3].
- Former state-sworn reserve officer with the New Haven Police Department (2005–2006); serves on the boards of Purdue University Extension Service and Northeast Indiana Crime Stoppers, according to the caucus [3].
- Moved to Fort Wayne in 1976 and graduated from Bishop Dwenger High School [3].

### Path to the ballot

- Unopposed in the May 5, 2026 Republican primary [10].

### Positions & plans

**Public safety**

- Says of the death penalty: "I do not believe that another human being should be taking a human life" [4]. Told 21Alive that if the law stands, "the same people who voted for it should fully understand what it means" [13].

**Energy & utilities**

- Said the March 2026 IURC energy-affordability investigation, which included Indiana Michigan Power, "adds another layer of accountability" to HEA 1002 [11].

**Economy & jobs**

- On HEA 1424: "Farmers and small business owners work hard to provide quality products to their communities" [6].

## Misti Meehan

Misti Meehan is a small-business owner who says she has worked more than a decade with the Allen County Election Board [15]. She was unopposed in the Democratic primary [10].

Campaign website: https://www.meehanfor84.com/

### Background

- Small-business owner; served in the Indiana Army National Guard [15].
- Says she has worked with the Allen County Election Board for over a decade [15].
- In 2019 she was chair of the Allen County Democratic Party and announced a run for the 1st District seat on the Fort Wayne City Council [16].
- Attended Northrop High School and IPFW; says she was born and raised in the community [15].

### Path to the ballot

- Unopposed in the May 5, 2026 Democratic primary [10].

### Positions & plans

**Economy & jobs**

- Says "too many working people are struggling while large corporations receive tax breaks and special treatment" and that businesses must succeed "but not on the backs of Hoosier families" [12, 17].

**Housing**

- Says "Families are being priced out of their own communities" [12].

**Child care**

- Says "childcare has become unaffordable or unavailable for many" [12].

### On the current officeholder

- No statements about Morris or his record were found on her website [17].

### How they say they'd run the office

- Says she wants "practical solutions that lower costs, strengthen our local economy" [12] and to bring "practical, bipartisan leadership back to the Statehouse" [17].
