## Adam W Henson

Republican nominee and sergeant with the Franklin County Sheriff's Office, where he has worked since 1996 [7].

### Background

- Sergeant with the Franklin County Sheriff's Office (promoted 2017); began as a jail officer in 1996 and became a deputy in 1999 [7].
- Supervises the office's evening shifts and administers the Indiana Traffic Safety Grant (Operation Pull Over); has been a law-enforcement instructor since 2004, a K-9 handler and a field training officer [7].
- Earlier worked with the Vincennes University Police Department; associate degree in law enforcement from Vincennes University; Indiana Law Enforcement Academy graduate (1999) [7].
- Received the office's Medal of Valor and Life Saving Award in 2022 [7].

### Path to the ballot
