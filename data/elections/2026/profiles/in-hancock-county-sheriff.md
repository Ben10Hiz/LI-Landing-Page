## Current officeholder: Brad Burkhart (Not confirmed in fetched sources)

Brad Burkhart is Hancock County sheriff [2] and is not on the November ballot [3]. As chief deputy in 2018 he spoke publicly about overcrowding in the county's old jail [4]. The year he took office and his party affiliation were not confirmed in fetched sources.

- February 2018: As chief deputy, Burkhart said the old jail was out of compliance with Department of Correction standards for staffing and population space; it was built for 157 inmates and held 198 on the day of the report. The office offered public jail tours ahead of a May 2018 property-tax referendum for a new jail [4].
- July 2019: The county council raised the county income tax from 1.74% to 1.94% to fund a new 400-bed jail and the added staffing it requires [5].
- 2021: A new jail of about 117,000 square feet with 440 beds and shell space for 136 more was completed east of Greenfield at a cost of about $38 million, according to its architect [6].
